Project overview (one-line)
Build a Retrieval-Augmented-Generation (RAG)-based centralized Contract Hub that (1) stores and retrieves contract knowledge, (2) generates draft contracts from pre-approved legal templates, and (3) validates generated or uploaded contracts against the corresponding business proposals.
Goals & success criteria
Single source of truth for contract clauses, templates and signed contracts.
Accurate draft generation using approved templates and the correct variables/clause choices.
Automated validation that checks a contract’s alignment with the business proposal (scope, price, SLAs, parties, timelines, risk flags).
Traceability / audit trail for every generated or modified contract and validation decision.
Minimized hallucinations and high explainability for any LLM outputs.

High-level architecture (components)
1.Ingestion & Catalog
oUpload/ingest contracts, templates, SOWs, proposals, policies, playbooks (PDFs, DOCX, text).
oExtract text + metadata, OCR where needed, store originals.
2.Knowledge Layer
oPreprocessing pipelines: chunking, cleaning, metadata tagging (contract type, clauses, parties, dates).
oEmbeddings service: compute embedding vectors for chunks and metadata.
3.Vector store / Index
oVector DB to store embeddings and enable nearest-neighbor retrieval for RAG.
4.RAG Engine (retriever + generator)
oRetriever: fetch relevant chunks given the user prompt or proposal context.
oGenerator: LLM that consumes retrieved evidence + template + prompt to produce a draft.
5.Template Engine
oPre-approved contract templates with parameter placeholders, conditional clause logic, and versioning.
oTemplate renderer merges LLM suggestions with structured placeholders; keeps approved wording as default.
6.Validation & Rules Engine
oMulti-layer validation:
Rule-based checks (regex, schema checks, numeric thresholds).
Clause detection & semantic matching (compare clauses to proposal facts via embeddings + similarity).
LLM-based validation to explain mismatches and suggest remediations.
oRisk scoring & flags.
7.UI / UX
oDashboard: search, create new contract from template, review drafts, validation reports, signoff workflow.
oSide-by-side view: generated contract vs. business proposal with highlighted mismatches.

oImmutable audit trail of generation prompts, retrieval snippets, LLM outputs, user edits, approvals.
oVersion control for templates & contracts.
9.Integrations
oCRM / ERP for business proposals (pull proposal data).
oE-signature provider (DocuSign, Adobe Sign).
oIDP / Authentication (SSO, RBAC).
oExisting CLM (if any) and document storage.

Prompt example (conceptual)
SYSTEM: You are a legal drafting assistant. Use only the provided evidence snippets and the approved template text. When inserting clauses, do not change statutory language labeled "approved". Provide an explanation for each clause chosen.

USER: Proposal: {structured data}
Template: {template text}
Evidence snippets: [{id, text}...]

TASK: Produce a draft contract filling placeholders and listing evidence citations per clause.

Prompt, retrieval, and hallucination mitigations (practical)
Always show source snippet citations in UI.
Enforce "approved wording" constraint in the prompt and implement post-processing that prevents unauthorized edits to mandatory clauses.
Use retrieval filtering (date, jurisdiction, document type) to restrict evidence set.
When confidence is low, require human review — surface the confidence and reason.
Keep a “gold” clause library for high-risk language that cannot be changed without legal approval.

Validation strategy (how to check correctness)
Unit tests for template rendering and simple rule checks.
Retrieval evaluation: precision@k, mean reciprocal rank using labeled relevance.
Validation evaluation: on annotated set of proposals/contracts, compute precision/recall of detected mismatches.
Human-in-the-loop acceptance: track percentage of drafts accepted as-is, average number of legal edits, and time to final contract.
